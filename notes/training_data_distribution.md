# icl-dataset training data distribution ("expanded" arm)

Covers the language-conditioned data mix used by every `*_expanded*` `openpi`
`TrainConfig` (`yor_icl_pi05_expanded*`, `yor_icl_victr_vision_expanded*`,
`yor_icl_victr_value_expanded*`, `yor_icl_fast_victr_*_expanded*`) — i.e. the
broader 31-task VICTR experiment, not the original 4-task left-arm pnp subset
(see `notes/training_runs.md`'s "Original 4-task subset" section for that
one, which uses a different `episodes_path`).

Generated by joining `assets/yor_icl_expanded_episodes.json` (the fixed
training episode set every `*_expanded*` `TrainConfig` reads via
`episodes_path`) against `icl-dataset`'s own `meta/episodes` parquet shards,
keyed by `episode_index`. Language prompts and the task-exclusion list are
`viktr.data.icl_dataset`'s `EXPANDED_PROMPT_OVERRIDES` /
`EXPANDED_TASKS_EXCLUDED` (`src/viktr/data/icl_dataset.py`) — byte-identical
to `openpi`'s own copy, `YOR_EXPANDED_TASK_PROMPT_OVERRIDES`
(`third_party/openpi/src/openpi/training/config.py`).

## Summary

- **31 of 36** dataset tasks used (5 excluded — see below).
- **1,784 training episodes**, **1,019,948 frames** total (matches the frame
  count in `shells/slurm/victr_precompute_frame_cache_job.sh`'s sizing
  comment).
- Per-task training-episode counts are **48, 50, or 100** — not uniform: most
  tasks get 50, five get 100 (the higher-volume original-collection tasks),
  and eight land at 48 (a few episodes short of 50 after
  `icl_dataset_v2_excluded_episodes.json`'s fail/invalid + per-task-balancing
  drops — see that file's `note` field, or `icl_dataset.py`'s
  `_excluded_episode_indices` docstring, for why).
- **8 of 31** tasks have their training prompt overridden from the raw
  dataset task string (clutter-mention removal, arm-qualifier rewording, or a
  fully-respecified instruction for two originally underspecified tasks —
  see `EXPANDED_PROMPT_OVERRIDES`'s docstring in `icl_dataset.py` for the
  per-category rationale). The other 23 train on the raw task string
  verbatim.

**Excluded from the expanded set** (`EXPANDED_TASKS_EXCLUDED`): `assemble the
two gears`, `open the notebook`, `put the green pepper into the grocery bag`,
`put the red chilli into the grocery bag`, `stack the colored octagons`.
